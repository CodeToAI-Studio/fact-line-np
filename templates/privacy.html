Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-12

What we collect

Fact Line NP does not require an account to read the news. We collect minimal data needed to run the site reliably, including:

  • Standard web logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) used for security and to understand what stories readers find useful.
  • A small cookie (flnp_viewed) that remembers which articles you have viewed, so our "Most Read" rankings are accurate. It contains no personal information.

How we use it

We use this information only to operate and improve the site — never to sell or share your data with advertisers or third parties. News tips you send us are treated confidentially and used only for reporting.
