About Fact Line NP

Our Mission

Fact Line NP is a Nepali digital news platform committed to verified, timely, and responsible journalism. We cover Nepal and international news with clarity and independence.

Every story on Fact Line NP is verified by at least two independent sources before publication. We believe accurate information is the foundation of an informed society.

Editorial Standards

We clearly label Opinion, Sponsored, Video, Interview, and Explainer content. Corrections are published promptly when errors are identified. We separate news reporting from editorial commentary.
